Terms of use

FixTrail is a hackathon project, provided as open-source software under the MIT License.

What FixTrail is

A record-keeping tool for repair problems in a rental home. It records reports, emails, responses, and photos, with server timestamps and tamper-evident fingerprints.

What FixTrail is not

  • Not legal advice. Tenant and landlord rights, notice requirements, and deadlines vary by location and lease. Some require specific notice methods, such as certified mail.
  • Not proof that an email was read. FixTrail can show an email was sent, not that it was opened. Emails can land in spam.
  • Not a guarantee of evidence. Whether a record is accepted is up to the relevant court, mediator, or agency.
  • Not able to verify everything. Calls, texts, and visits that FixTrail didn't witness are labeled "tenant-reported" or "landlord-reported".

Using it fairly

Use FixTrail only for real repair issues at a property you rent or own, and don't attempt to alter or falsify the record. Every report is sent to the other party.
